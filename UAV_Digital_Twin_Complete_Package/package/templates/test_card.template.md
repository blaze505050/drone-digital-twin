## Safety approval — complete before energizing equipment

Hazards; rated equipment and guards/restraint; manufacturer operating limits; battery precautions; exclusion zone; qualified supervision; stop criteria; emergency procedure; responsible operator. Ground bench energy cutoff and in-flight failsafe are separate cases. No unreviewed propeller-on or autonomous flight procedure is authorized by this template.

## Instrumentation

Instrument IDs, ranges, accuracy/uncertainty, calibration, mounting/frame, sample rates, clocks/alignment, reference quality, saturation and dropout checks.

## Procedure

Initial conditions; controlled variables; independent variables; planned operating points; repeat count and ordering; thermal settling; allowed deviations; logging checks; shut-down procedure.

## Data and evaluation

Raw file hashes; channel roles; source status; calibration/training/tuning/test grouping; forecast issue-time information; exclusions fixed in advance; baseline versions; metrics and normalization; uncertainty method; acceptance thresholds and rationale.

## Results

Include all runs, exclusions/failures, plots/units, measurement uncertainty, per-run metrics, confidence intervals and comparison to baseline. Mark PASS / FAIL / NOT_SCORED per requirement; do not turn a skipped or missing-data test into PASS.

## Decision and limitations

What engineering decision follows? Where may the result be used? What remains unsupported? Parameter/configuration promotion approved or rejected, with reason. Reproduction command and output artifact IDs. Operator/reviewer sign-off and unresolved deviations.
