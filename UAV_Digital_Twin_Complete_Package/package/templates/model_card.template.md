## Equations and assumptions

State/input/output definitions; units/frames/reference points; governing equations; numerical method and step; boundary conditions; geometry/material approximations; parameter definitions and source; known omitted physics.

## Data and calibration

Manifests and licences; measured vs synthetic status; instruments/uncertainty; training/tuning/test splits; identifiable parameters and fixed priors; fitting method; residual model; parameter covariance and physical bounds.

## Validation and applicability

Independent reference; matched conditions; evidence artifacts; metrics including failures and uncertainty; operating domain; extrapolation policy; baseline comparisons; numerical verification; initial/reseeding and forecast input-information policy.

## Operational constraints

Runtime hardware/latency; dependency versions; invalid/NaN/nonconvergence handling; stale-data behaviour; candidate/active/rollback policy; no-authority or explicitly approved control role.

## Claims and limitations

Supported claims with evidence links; unsupported claims; uncertainty calibration/coverage; correlated-data assumptions; recommended and prohibited uses; remaining tests; reviewer/date.
